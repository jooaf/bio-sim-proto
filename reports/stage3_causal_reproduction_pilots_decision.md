# Stage 3 causal reproduction pilots decision

## Gate A: vacancy-first opportunity — GO

The vacancy-first protocol passed every frozen mechanics criterion:

- 6/6 runs successful, exactly conserved, and invariant-clean;
- 3/3 mechanically feasible runs at each radius;
- zero no-parent blocks and zero pool blocks;
- median births 78 at both radii;
- treatment median birth ratio exactly 1.000;
- median late occupancy 0.848 at both radii.

The protocol removes the observed birth-opportunity asymmetry from the parent-first campaign under this operating point. A matched neutral lineage-patch confirmation may proceed with unchanged settings and unseen seeds.

No family or spatial-composition endpoint was inspected in this pilot.

## Gate B: execution-gated exact-copy birth — NO-GO

The byte-exact trigger was absent:

- exact-copy triggers: 0 in all three 10,000-tick runs;
- trigger-gated births: 0 in all runs;
- all runs were otherwise mechanically feasible, conserved, and invariant-clean.

This is a mechanism-availability failure, not a software or liveness failure. Under the frozen BFF conditions, ordinary interactions did not produce the specified exact source-preserving full-tape copy event.

The criterion will not be weakened, the horizon and seeds will not be extended, and mutation or interaction parameters will not be tuned to rescue it. Gate B is closed as a negative result.

## Consequences

- Scheduled copy birth remains explicitly exogenous.
- Vacancy-controlled lineage placement and persistence studies may continue as mechanism controls.
- Energy coupling may be implemented and tested with scheduled birth, but cannot be described as endogenous reproduction.
- A future substrate with independently established exact-copy viability would require a new preregistration rather than reopening this pilot.
