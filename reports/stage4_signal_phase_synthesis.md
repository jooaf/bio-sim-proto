# Stage 4 structured-signal phase synthesis

## Decision

The minimum viable **read-only structured-signal phase is complete**.

Three independent held-out gates establish the required causal stack:

1. **Exact event dispatch:** matched tags dispatched every interaction; mismatched
   and disabled controls did not (`S4S-E001`).
2. **Spatial conditional response:** one immutable dual-handler tape executed
   uptake only in the spatial half carrying its uptake tag, with uniform and
   disabled controls (`S4S-E003`).
3. **Task-relevant opportunity:** declared-correct responders received a
   0.794–0.804 interaction share versus 0.491–0.508 without task weighting;
   paired effects were positive in 10/10 seeds (`S4S-E004`).

All three gates preserved tape immutability and exact energy/matter accounting.
The full implementation suite passed after each model change.

## Negative boundaries

Writable partner-cell signaling failed both its original random-occupancy gate
and a separately preregistered fully connected positive control. Writers changed
the tags that enabled their own dispatch and deactivated before field completion.
Those failures remain authoritative: this phase does not support signal writing,
communication, coordination, or niche construction.

Energy-coupled scheduled reproduction also failed its frozen per-run liveness
gate despite uniformly directional frequency effects. No reproduction or
frequency-change claim is accepted.

## Supported claim

A fixed program can use exact local environmental tags to select different
handlers, express spatially conditional behavior, and receive increased future
interaction opportunity when its response satisfies an explicit task rule.

## Unsupported claims

The evidence does not establish endogenous reproduction, evolved adaptation,
communication, coordination, competition, trophic ecology, empirical
organization, self-maintenance, multicellularity, or organism identity.

## Stop decision

Additional nearby parameter sweeps would not resolve the main uncertainty and
risk tuning around failed gates. The next work should begin from a separately
reviewed phase design that supplies a non-artificial path from interaction
opportunity to endogenous persistence or reproduction, or proceeds to larger-
scale signal-correlation and organization measurements with explicit positive
controls. No further Stage 4 campaign is currently justified.
