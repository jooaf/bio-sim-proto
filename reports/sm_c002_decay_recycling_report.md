## Material fate and exposure

Both arms began with the same 8,960 molecular bytes and zero accessible pool or
waste. Both experienced native decay:

- recycle: 333–481 nonzero decay events, ending with 3,617–5,500 molecular bytes,
  3,460–5,343 accessible bytes, and zero waste;
- sequester: 121–136 nonzero decay events, ending with 256–1,216 molecular bytes,
  zero accessible bytes, and 7,744–8,704 waste bytes. Sequester recorded zero
accepted changing copies as well as zero positive-growth bytes.

The different later decay counts and populations are downstream consequences of
the randomized routing intervention, not matched death schedules. Recycle ended
with populations 293–484. Sequester ended with 4–19 surviving founders and no
successful birth. Neither arm recorded a failed placement, and even initial
population plus every recycle birth remained below the 1,600-cell capacity, so a
vacancy shortage does not explain the contrast.

Zero sequester births is an observed conditional result, not a universal
impossibility theorem. It does not mean cleavage always copies or that an
individual birth creates matter. Whole-parent cleavage can transfer a complete
sequence without net replication. All 12,656 observed recycle births increased
population by one at placement, but this does not prove that each child was a
faithful copied replicator or reproductively competent.

## Interpretation

SM-C002 establishes more than a correlation between scarcity blocks and outcome:
the decay event itself remained native, while only the destination and subsequent
accessibility of its exactly conserved symbols changed. Accessible turnover
supported thousands of late growth bytes and hundreds of late births; inertly
sequestered turnover supported neither.

The intervention also produces downstream occupancy, encounter, and mortality
differences. Those are part of the total causal effect of material accessibility,
not separately identified mechanisms. The whole-run funding lower bound is accounting evidence, not atom-level
provenance or proof that a particular late birth used material from a particular
late decay. The pool is matter, not energy, and its return is an externally
defined routing rule rather than evolved or regulated metabolism.
