# BFF persistence failure and future comparative-language question

**Recorded:** 2026-09-18

## What failed—and what did not

The conserved BFF work did **not** fail physical conservation. Every accepted
campaign preserved exact per-symbol soup-plus-pool totals. Mechanics, provenance
instrumentation, and accounting passed their parity and integrity gates.

What failed was sustained biological organization at the lineage level:

- exact score-64 witnesses amplified strongly but disappeared before the frozen
  persistence window (AC-P006);
- provenance-aware, non-exact score-64 descendants occurred transiently and beat
  composition-preserving controls in all nine held-out pairs (FR-P001);
- nevertheless, every witness and control had zero final non-exact descendant
  abundance, and 0/9 witnesses met sustained final sampled presence;
- energy-coupled endogenous reproduction and writable signaling had already
  failed their independent frozen gates.

Thus BFF supports exact conservation, strict functional origins, composition-
level convergence, and sequence-specific transient propagation, but not persistent
lineage, heredity, maintenance, ecology, or organism identity under the tested
protocols.

## Plausible substrate-level contributors

These are hypotheses, not established causes:

1. **No atomic birth event.** BFF interactions overwrite two fixed-size tapes in
   place. A copied pattern does not create a separately identified offspring.
2. **No native lineage identity.** Parent–offspring relationships must be inferred
   from bytes or observational provenance; identity is not part of the mechanics.
3. **Destructive shared execution.** Programs and data occupy the same joint tape,
   so execution, mutation, and partner writes can erase a propagating pattern
   without a protected birth boundary.
4. **Fixed population slots.** Propagation redistributes sequence states rather
   than increasing a lineage through explicit cleavage into a new individual.
5. **Global conserved-pool coupling.** Exact matter conservation makes write order
   and symbol availability globally observable. This can impose sequence-specific
   scarcity without supplying the reproductive individuality needed for ecology.
6. **Proxy/function separation.** Paper score 64 is a stable-position copying
   proxy. Even combined with value-change provenance, it does not prove that the
   inherited bytes cause future reproduction.

The experiments do not isolate which factor is decisive. In particular, they do
not show that conservation itself destroys ecology.

## Deferred comparative investigation

A future study could test whether BFF-like language semantics systematically
produce transient propagation or ecological collapse under conservation, compared
with substrates that have explicit offspring and lineage identity.

Candidate comparison classes include:

- other minimal in-place tape languages or Forth-like primordial soups;
- Funge/FALSE-style self-modifying languages;
- Tierra/Avida-like instruction systems with explicit allocation/division;
- Stringmol with binding, copy, cleavage, and individual IDs; and
- non-string chemistries such as SKI, provided reproduction units are defined
  prospectively.

The useful design is factorial rather than a language leaderboard:

1. in-place overwrite versus atomic offspring creation;
2. inferred versus native lineage identity;
3. unconserved versus exactly matter-conserved execution;
4. fixed slots versus explicit population birth/death;
5. well-mixed versus local interaction and offspring placement; and
6. matched mutation burden, resource pressure, and computational opportunity.

Primary outcomes should separate origin, reproduction, lineage persistence,
heredity fidelity, population regulation, ecological coexistence, and conservation
integrity. Positive controls must be established within each substrate before any
cross-language ecological comparison.

## Current decision

This investigation is deliberately deferred. The immediate pivot is SM-L001:
validate native Stringmol cleavage birth and individual lineage identity before
considering a separately preregistered conservation boundary.
