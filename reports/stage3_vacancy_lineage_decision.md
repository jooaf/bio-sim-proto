# Stage 3 vacancy-controlled lineage decision

## Decision

**GO: local parent selection and offspring displacement cause neutral lineage clustering when vacancy opportunity and realized birth count are matched.**

Primary radius-1 minus radius-8 family-neighbor excess:

- mean `+0.045032`;
- median `+0.045634`;
- 95% paired bootstrap interval `[+0.041877, +0.047977]`;
- exact one-sided sign-flip `p = 0.000977`;
- positive effects in 10/10 matched seeds.

## Opportunity and liveness controls

- Median births were 171.5 in both treatments; ratio `1.000`.
- Each matched seed had exactly the same successful birth count across radii.
- No-parent blocks: zero in both treatments.
- Pool blocks: zero in both treatments.
- Median late occupancy: 0.893 in both treatments.
- Mechanical feasibility: 10/10 per treatment.
- Exact symbol conservation: 20/20.
- Invariant failures: zero.

The original parent-first confound is therefore absent under the preregistered vacancy-first protocol.

## Secondary agreement

- Mean exact-root neighbor excess was `+0.049811` at radius 1 and `+0.001211` at radius 8.
- Mean neutral-family excess was `+0.045572` at radius 1 and `+0.000540` at radius 8.
- All 10 local within-run family tests were significant; none of the wide tests were.
- Live reproductive-descendant fractions were identical at `0.175` treatment means.

The effect is not explained by more births, more descendants, unequal vacancies, or modulo-family collision alone.

## Supported claim

> In the scheduled conserved-copy model, selecting parents close to uniformly sampled vacancies produces reproducible neutral lineage patches.

## Limits

Birth remains scheduler-initiated. The separate exact-copy interaction gate failed with zero triggers and remains a NO-GO. These results do not establish endogenous reproduction, selection, organisms, self-maintenance, or energy ecology.
