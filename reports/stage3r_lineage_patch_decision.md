# Stage 3R neutral lineage-patch decision

## Decision

**The preregistered neutral lineage-patch mechanism passed. This is not an organism-emergence result.**

## Primary result

Placement-radius-1 minus placement-radius-8 neutral-family neighbor excess:

- mean paired effect: **+0.032600**;
- median paired effect: **+0.032773**;
- 95% paired bootstrap interval: **[+0.031180, +0.033990]**;
- exact one-sided sign-flip: **p = 0.000977**;
- positive matched effects: **10/10**.

All frozen criteria passed. Every run completed, conserved all byte counts exactly, remained mechanically feasible, and recorded zero invariant failures.

The exact-root secondary result agrees with the modulo-16 family result, reducing concern that the effect is solely a family-label collision artifact.

## Important treatment asymmetry

Radius-1 placement produced 448 aggregate no-space blocks and fewer births, while radius-8 placement produced none. Consequently, placement radius changes both offspring displacement and access to vacancies. The result demonstrates the complete local-placement mechanism, not a pure displacement effect at an experimentally equal birth count.

Despite fewer descendants, local placement produced much stronger neighbor clustering. This direction argues against the effect being explained merely by greater reproductive abundance.

## Supported claim

> Under scheduled pool-funded copy birth, local offspring placement creates reproducible neutral lineage clustering under exact symbol conservation.

## Unsupported claims

The experiment does not establish:

- endogenous BFF self-replication;
- adaptive fitness or selection;
- organizational closure or self-maintenance;
- organism identity;
- the roadmap's energy-ledger Stage 3 gate;
- persistence beyond 10,000 ticks.

Families were offline neutral labels (`root tape ID mod 16`) and never affected chemistry.

## Next model ideas

1. **Vacancy-opportunity control:** compare local and wide placement while matching successful birth counts or providing equal candidate vacancy sets.
2. **Endogenous birth trigger:** require a documented interaction-derived copy criterion rather than scheduler-selected neutral cloning.
3. **Energy coupling:** fund birth from a real energy ledger while retaining exact symbol conservation.
4. **Lineage patch lifetime:** measure family-cluster survival over longer horizons and after local birth is switched off.
5. **Organizational analysis:** identify closed, self-maintaining interaction sets without declaring neutral families to be organisms.

## Very-long Stage 2 run decision

A new 500,000-tick matched-radius Stage 2 campaign is **not required now**. The unchanged Stage 2 locality mechanism has replicated at 5,000 and 50,000 ticks, predicts standardized execution behavior, and shows spatial distance decay. The next uncertainty is causal birth ecology, which a Stage 2 duration extension cannot answer.
