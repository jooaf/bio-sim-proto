# Stage 3 energy-funded birth decision

## Decision

**GO: parent-held energy causally constrains scheduled conserved birth.**

Cost-0 minus cost-5 successful births:

- mean paired effect `43.9` births;
- median `43.5`;
- 95% paired bootstrap interval `[38.598, 48.900]`;
- exact one-sided sign-flip `p = 0.000977`;
- positive effects in 10/10 pairs.

## Controls and mechanics

- Cost-0 positive control median: 84 births.
- Cost-5 median: 38 births, 45% of control.
- Energy-blocked attempts occurred in 10/10 cost-5 runs, 473 total.
- Cost-5 remained viable above the frozen minimum of ten median births.
- Mechanical feasibility: 20/20.
- Exact symbol conservation: 20/20.
- Maximum relative energy error: `1.945e-11`.
- Pool blocks and invariant failures: zero.

The birth transaction was atomic: failed energy checks changed neither energy nor symbols.

## Supported claim

> Under the selected uniform energy flow, a fixed parent-held energy expenditure limits otherwise identical scheduled copy births while preserving explicit energy and matter accounting.

## Limits

Parents acquire energy by passive local absorption from a uniform field. Birth remains scheduler-initiated, and the model contains no differentiated resource conversion, predation, trophic structure, or endogenous copy trigger. The exact-copy trigger gate separately failed and remains closed.

The result validates energetic cost, not ecological fitness or organisms.
