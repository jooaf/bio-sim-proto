# Failed and mixed experiment registry

**Updated:** 2026-09-12

## Purpose

This registry keeps negative results visible. A failed frozen gate is not erased
by later positive controls, reanalysis, renamed endpoints, or nearby parameter
changes. Each entry states what failed, what remains supported, and which follow-
ups are prohibited unless opened as an explicitly new design.

## S0-E001 — Tractable 256-tape emergence

- **Decision:** FAIL as an emergence test; mechanics passed.
- **Failed criterion:** replication appeared in 1/20 runs and no required
  diversity collapse occurred.
- **Diagnosis:** the protocol was orders of magnitude smaller than the cited BFF
  paper and initially omitted paper-style disjoint pairing and mutation.
- **Retained:** deterministic BFF execution and logging mechanics.
- **Resolution:** S0-E002 independently reproduced emergence at paper scale; it
  did not convert S0-E001 into a pass.

## S1-E003 — Conservation effect on emergence onset

- **Decision:** Mixed; onset hypothesis failed.
- **Failed criterion:** conserved multiplier-2 and unconserved controls each
  emerged in 1/5 runs; no detectable onset effect.
- **Retained:** scarcity strongly controlled blocked writes, and adjacent
  matter-flow windows retained structure above a null.
- **Boundary:** do not claim that ordinary matched conservation accelerates or
  suppresses origin from this experiment.

## S1-E004 — Strong class-specific origin-filter claim

- **Decision:** Suggestive, not confirmatory.
- **Failed criterion:** natural-six exclusion produced 0/5 origins versus 3/5
  control (`p=0.083`), while the unrelated-symbol control generated materially
  less blocked-write load.
- **Retained:** established ecologies buffered severe deprivation; origin
  suppression and symbol specificity are credible leads.
- **Boundary:** generic friction remains a live confound. This motivates AC-I001
  but is not itself a confirmed class-specific filter.

## S2-E003 — BFF parasite positive control and integrated containment

- **Decision:** NO-GO; candidate rejected.
- **Failed criterion:** the frozen BFF copier did not reliably invade at large
  radius under the actual mutation regime; ancestry increased in only 1/5 runs.
- **Retained:** strict mutation-free copy mechanics worked, and an external
  Spatial Stringmol host/parasite control later validated locality containment.
- **Boundary:** do not claim BFF parasite containment or tune family definitions,
  mutation, insertion count, or horizon around this candidate.

## Integrated Phase 2 acceptance

- **Decision:** NO-GO.
- **Failed criterion:** the 500,000-tick run's opcode-signature q=1 block-beta
  statistic was not significant (`p=0.127`), despite passing every mechanical
  criterion and positional byte-identity (`p=0.001`).
- **Retained:** exact long-run conservation, liveness, anti-clogging, and
  persistent local byte-level similarity.
- **Boundary:** the completed gate must not be converted to a pass by dropping or
  replacing the frozen beta criterion. New science proceeds under a new design.

## S3-E002 — Execution-gated exact-copy birth availability

- **Decision:** NO-GO.
- **Failed criterion:** three frozen 10,000-tick runs produced zero exact-copy
  triggers and zero births.
- **Retained:** trigger detection and conservation mechanics.
- **Boundary:** do not weaken byte-exact copying or call scheduled cloning
  endogenous reproduction.

## S3-E005 — Lineage switch-off integrated gate

- **Decision:** Integrated NO-GO.
- **Failed criterion:** the continued-birth positive control clogged; only 1/10
  controls satisfied the occupancy ceiling.
- **Retained:** stopped-arm neutral lineage patches persisted strongly.
- **Resolution:** a separately preregistered density-independent regulation
  experiment later passed. The original clogged gate remains failed.

## S3-E006 — Empirical organization excess

- **Decision:** representation passed; organization excess unsupported.
- **Failed criterion:** accepted organizations appeared in 5/15 windows, below
  the frozen 8/15 prevalence threshold.
- **Retained:** composition species were identifiable and observed organizations
  exceeded the product-permutation null where present.
- **Boundary:** no self-maintenance, intervention, or organism claim is licensed.

## S4-E005 — Resource-coupled birth and frequency change

- **Decision:** FAIL the frozen confirmation.
- **Failed criterion:** two low-frequency enabled runs recorded fewer than the
  required 16 births; the minimum was 3.
- **Retained:** frequency change was positive in 30/30 cases and all successful
  enabled births had uptake parents, as descriptive evidence only.
- **Boundary:** no resource-coupled reproduction claim and no nearby birth-rate,
  placement, or horizon tuning.

## S4S-E002 — Writable partner-cell signals

- **Decision:** FAIL.
- **Failed criterion:** random half occupancy left isolated tapes; only 5–6 of 8
  occupied tags changed, and no run completed the field.
- **Retained:** reachable partner-cell writes were atomic, controls behaved
  correctly, tapes stayed immutable, and matter remained exact.
- **Boundary:** no writable-signal, communication, or niche-construction claim.

## S4S-R001 — Fully connected writable-signal control

- **Decision:** FAIL.
- **Failed criterion:** only 14–15 of 16 tags changed. Writers whose own tags were
  replaced stopped exact dispatch and stranded the remaining initial tags.
- **Retained:** the failure identified dynamic self-deactivation rather than
  static reachability.
- **Boundary:** writable signaling and the planned causal inter-tape response are
  stopped. The later read-only structured response does not overturn this.

## AC-I001 — Grid-based nonspecific-friction calibration

- **Decision:** FAIL; confirmation not launched.
- **Failed criterion:** no preregistered friction rate matched natural-six blocked
  load within `[0.8, 1.25]`; the closest rate, 0.08, reached only 0.280 of the
  natural-six median.
- **Retained:** all 30 mechanics runs succeeded with exact conservation;
  natural-six deprivation blocked 24.9–32.3 million changing writes in 5,000
  epochs. Emergence metrics were not inspected.
- **Boundary:** do not expand the frozen rate grid and call it AC-I001. Any
  replacement calibration requires a new design and new seeds.

## Cross-cutting lessons

1. Positive mechanics do not imply ecological or organizational function.
2. A failed positive control blocks interpretation even when a treatment contrast
   looks favorable.
3. Descriptive directional effects below a conjunction gate remain descriptive.
4. New experiments may address a diagnosed confound only with new IDs, held-out
   seeds, and preregistration; the original decision never changes.
5. The next discovery phase targets BFF conserved-chemistry origin filtering,
   not retries of failed Phase 2 spatial metrics or Stage 4 writable signaling.
