# Functional, distance-decay, and reproduction research synthesis

## Requested tests and outcomes

### 1. Does opcode composition predict execution behavior?

**Passed.** In a preregistered four-probe standardized assay, the mean seed-level Mantel Spearman association was `0.284521`, with 95% bootstrap interval `[0.259729, 0.309913]`, exact one-sided `p = 0.000977`, and positive results in 10/10 matched seed averages. All 20 individual run tests were significant.

Interpretation: composition carries moderate information about controlled execution potential. The unconstrained assay is not realized fitness.

### 2. Is there a spatial distance-decay curve?

**Passed.** Radius-1 interaction produced stronger decline of opcode-composition similarity with toroidal distance than radius 8. The mean paired decay-excess effect was `+0.00013480` similarity units/cell, 95% interval `[+0.00010728, +0.00016145]`, exact `p = 0.000977`, positive in 10/10 pairs. Both primary hypotheses passed Holm correction.

Interpretation: the local-composition result is spatially graded, not merely a nearest-neighbor artifact. Absolute slopes are small.

### 3. Explicit conserved reproduction and lineage patch tests

**Mechanism implemented and neutral patch control passed.** Stage 3R adds scheduled pool-funded exact-copy birth, local offspring placement, and one-parent lineage. It preserves exact byte conservation and is forced off in Stages 0–2. Energy-enabled Stage 3 is explicitly rejected as unimplemented.

A preregistered liveness pilot selected birth rate `2×10⁻⁵` without inspecting patch statistics. In the subsequent 20-run matched placement campaign:

- mean local-minus-wide neutral-family neighbor excess: `+0.032600`;
- 95% interval: `[+0.031180, +0.033990]`;
- exact `p = 0.000977`;
- positive effects: 10/10;
- all runs successful, conserved, feasible, and invariant-clean.

Interpretation: local scheduled birth causes neutral lineage clustering. This is not organism emergence, endogenous self-replication, selection, or organizational closure.

### 4. 500,000-tick matched-radius confirmation

**Not required by the current evidence or a defined future gate; deferred.** Continuous Stage 2 locality has already passed at 5,000 and 50,000 ticks on 20/20 matched pairs, and functional prediction plus distance decay independently passed. A further unchanged Stage 2 duration experiment would not answer the new causal question about birth ecology.

A 500,000-tick matched campaign should be preregistered only if a future protocol specifically requires very-long persistence in the unchanged Stage 2 model.

## Overall evidence chain

The completed evidence supports:

1. exact symbol conservation and long-run mechanical liveness;
2. radius-sensitive positional byte similarity;
3. replicated and temporally persistent opcode-composition similarity;
4. a moderate composition–execution association under standardized probes;
5. toroidal distance decay of composition similarity;
6. neutral lineage clustering under explicit local copy birth.

It does not yet support:

- the historical categorical opcode block-beta criterion;
- endogenous BFF reproduction;
- adaptive ecological selection;
- self-maintaining organizational closure;
- organism identity;
- the planned energy-ledger Stage 3.

## Recommended next phase

Stop tuning Stage 2. Treat its locality mechanism as established within the tested simulator conditions.

For model development, the next decisive step is to replace neutral scheduled birth with a preregistered interaction-derived birth trigger, or to implement the planned energy ledger and test whether reproduction can be funded by behaviorally acquired energy. Organism claims should wait for organizational-closure and self-maintenance analyses rather than using neutral lineage labels as organisms.
