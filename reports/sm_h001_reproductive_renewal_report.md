# SM-H001 descendant reproductive-renewal report

## Decision

**PASS.** All mechanics, integrity, inert-control, renewal-core, and inherited-
sequence-continuity gates passed. The same 18/20 host runs passed all six frozen
requirements, above the required 16/20.

In the pinned seeded conserved-recycle Stringmol host, noninitial descendants
themselves became the material source of later population-increasing births,
including descendants born at or after tick 2,500. Before their first such reproduction,
renewing descendants retained 0.984375–1.0 of their inherited birth bytes at the
same positions.

This establishes finite-horizon operational reproductive renewal and inherited
sequence continuity. It does not show that the inherited sequence is causally
necessary for function, functional heredity under perturbation, adaptation,
indefinite persistence, metabolism, self-maintenance, ecology, or organisms.

| Late renewing descendants | 2–18 | ≥5 |
| Retained late renewals | 2–18 | ≥5 |

Eighteen runs passed every component. Seed `202623001` had ten renewing
descendants but only two late/retained-late renewals. Seed `202623016` had eight
renewing descendants and three late/retained-late renewals. Both remained valid
non-passes in the fixed denominator.

Across all hosts, first-source inherited-byte retention was 0.984375–1.0. Renewal
birth lengths were 29–66 bytes; retained-late qualification required at least 32
bytes and retention at least 0.90. Source depth at least two means a native child
later produced another population-increasing child while surviving as a nonempty
source, not merely that it appeared as one of two ancestry labels.

The frozen 16/20 fair-coin reference tail is 0.00590897. The observed 18/20 is a
composite pass count, not a directional comparison; the reference tail is
illustrative and not a p-value for this composite gate.

## Controls and excluded native events

All ten inert controls recorded zero native births, productive source births,
renewing descendants, and source depth.

Across hosts, 12,647 of 12,654 native births qualified as productive source
births. One run recorded the other seven as whole-parent transfers; these were
valid native births but excluded from the
qualifying source lineage because their source did not survive. No orphan-source
productive birth occurred. The conservative rules therefore did not manufacture
renewal by assigning whole-transfer children an implementation-chosen depth.

Active/passive lineage labels did not determine source. The analyzer inferred the
single surviving source from stable contiguous suffix loss, verified exact ordered
child receipt, and reconstructed subsequent buffers before testing renewal and
retention.

## Interpretation

SM-H001 closes an important gap in the earlier native-lineage result. Persistence
was not solely an accumulation of inert descendants: numerous noninitial
individuals later served as surviving material sources for further
population-increasing births, and this continued among late-born cohorts.
Inherited birth sequences were almost completely retained before first source
reproduction.

The result is still observational with respect to sequence function. High
positional symbol agreement plus renewed reproduction does not prove continuous
preservation of particular physical bytes or that retained sequence causes
competence; partner context or unmeasured molecular state may matter. The next
experiment should prospectively perturb or composition-shuffle selected renewing
descendant sequences and assay exact versus control cohorts under standardized
partner/material conditions.
