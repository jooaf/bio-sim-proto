## What changed scientifically

The earlier lineage effect is no longer confounded by unequal vacancy access or successful birth count. Uniformly sampled vacancies and identical realized births still produced strong local-family and exact-root clustering.

Energy is now an explicit flow rather than a placeholder. It constrains instruction execution and scheduled birth while coexisting with exact symbol conservation. The implementation passes accounting invariants, but uniform passive absorption is not a trophic ecology.

The frozen exact-copy trigger failed completely. Therefore no current BFF result supports interaction-gated reproduction. Scheduled cloning must remain labeled exogenous.

The switch-off result is informative but does not pass its integrated gate. Lineage labels survived almost unchanged for 9,900 ticks without new births, consistent with slow turnover. Continued scheduled birth clogged the lattice, so long-run positive-control feasibility failed. This is not self-maintenance.

## Supported claims

1. Local parent selection causes neutral lineage clustering when vacancy and birth opportunities are matched.
2. Explicit energy can be numerically balanced alongside exact per-byte matter conservation.
3. Parent-held energy expenditure causally limits scheduled conserved birth.
4. Neutral lineage spatial labels can remain associated long after birth stops under the tested turnover rate.

## Unsupported claims

- viable execution-mediated exact copying in the tested BFF regime;
- trophic levels or predation;
- adaptive fitness;
- organizational closure or self-maintenance;
- organism identity;
- indefinitely viable scheduled-birth dynamics.
