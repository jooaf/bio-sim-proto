## Answer to the narrow question

Local placement accompanies fewer admitted children, distinct rooted renewing
nonfounders and late-born renewers under both fixed interaction settings. The
measured difference is therefore not limited to total population. However,
observed renewal fractions do not have a consistent direction across contexts.

| Cell | Renewers / admitted children | Late-born renewers / late-born children | Serial source events | Final exact HOST / total |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| GG |25/664 (3.765%)|6/308 (1.948%)|140|7/347|
| LG |47/539 (8.720%)|21/251 (8.367%)|190|25/230|
| GL |12/339 (3.540%)|2/90 (2.222%)|50|4/157|
| LL |39/413 (9.443%)|12/165 (7.273%)|126|15/187|

First letter is interaction, second placement; G=global, L=local. The primary
placement contrasts are GL-GG and LL-LG. Distinct renewers change by-13/-8 and
late-born renewers by-4/-9. Per-admitted-child renewal changes by-0.225/+0.723
percentage points; late-born fractions change by+0.274/-1.094points. Keep these
opposing directions, rather than pooling them into one locality effect.

All populations grew from140 while exact HOST declined from140. Admission/births,
source reuse and exact-sequence maintenance are not interchangeable. Late-born
means birth>=2500; late-acting means action>=2500. Every near-end birth remains
in the denominator: unequal follow-up, survival and opportunity prevent interpreting
fractions as intrinsic competence. Source role need not be active executor, and
sequence identity or ancestry is not an assay of standardized competence.

**Recommendation: measurement work before another population study.** Specify
which productive role and exposure constitute reproductive performance, and how
partner context and censoring will be represented, before considering a new
prospective hypothesis. Existing crowding/failure/renewal associations do not
establish causal mediation. No new study is designed or authorized here.
